Initial encounter loading:
AVAILABLE_ENEMIES is the reusable catalog of all enemy definitions, including enemies used only as companions or spawn targets.
ENEMY_POOL separately lists selectable starting groups using definitions from that catalog. Adding an enemy to AVAILABLE_ENEMIES alone does not make it a starting group.
Each starting group can list initial companions by catalog enemy ID. The starting group is the primary enemy followed by those companions.
Groups are: one Ashbound Initiate; two Glass Wardens; three Mireling Swarms; one Ironhowl Brute.
Companions and manually spawned enemies do not recursively add their own companions.
Get enemies fills each row once per load with a randomly chosen group, without reusing a group in another row.
Unseen groups are used before restarting the pool. Clearing defeated rows preserves this history and enables another load.
Get enemies is disabled after loading. No spawn buttons appear before loading or in the overview.
An optional companions array defines the initial group; an optional spawnPool array defines manual spawn targets. Missing or empty arrays mean no companions or no spawning, respectively.
Both companions and spawnPool resolve IDs against AVAILABLE_ENEMIES, not the starting groups in ENEMY_POOL.
Keep spawnPool on a catalog definition when every instance of that enemy should spawn; a starting group can override it for its primary enemy.
Leaving and starting a new game resets loading controls and group history.
